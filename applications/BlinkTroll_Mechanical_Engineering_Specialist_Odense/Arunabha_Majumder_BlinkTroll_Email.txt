I would like to apply for the Mechanical Engineering Specialist role in Odense. I have attached my CV and a motivation letter.

I am a mechanical engineer finishing my PhD in Mechatronics and Robotics at Aalborg University, and I finish in March. For the last 3 years my main project has been a shoulder exoskeleton. The design idea was mine: a spring carries most of the arm load, so a small 6 Nm motor is enough to do the rest. I sketched it by hand, modelled it in SolidWorks, made the 2D drawings and then 3D printed and rebuilt the parts many times until they fitted well and held up in testing. It cut energy use by more than 25% and won the Best Paper Award at IFToMM ISRM 2026.

A big part of that work was making the mechanics play well with everything else. I fitted the motor, encoder and force sensors into the frame and worked closely with the control and software people to get it running as one system. For our arm studies I built an Arduino-based trigger to sync a 5-bar robot with the Qualisys motion capture cameras. Test days with real participants always meant some troubleshooting on the spot, and I actually enjoy that part. Back in my Bachelor's I did the machining and assembly of a rehab exoskeleton myself, so I am comfortable with tools and the workshop.

I also have a Diploma in Fine Arts, so sketching an idea quickly so the whole team sees the same thing comes naturally to me. That fits well with a small team of hardware, software and embedded engineers like yours.
