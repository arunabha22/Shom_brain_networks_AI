# Hiring-contact research routine (saved for Arunabha, 2026-11-03)

Use this when Arunabha sends a job (company, title, URL, location, domain). Do not guess identities or emails.

1. Read the official job page: department, team, product, reporting clues, location, requisition ID, keywords.
2. Search public web / LinkedIn-indexed posts for: "my team is hiring", "we are hiring", "join my team",
   the exact job title, the team or product name.
3. Up to three possible contacts; prefer someone linking the exact role or naming the same team; confirm they
   currently work at the company.
4. Grade each: A = exact role/team + explicit hiring statement; B = same function/adjacent role + explicit hiring
   statement; C = title-based possibility, no direct hiring evidence.
5. Quote at most one short sentence per post; give post URL, date, current title, why it matches.
6. Never call anyone "the hiring manager" unless public evidence says so; use "possible team lead" or
   "relevant recruiting contact".
7. Only if a Hunter tool is connected: Email Finder with official domain + name/handle; accept only a verified
   professional work email. Never invent an address, expose an API key or return personal contact info.
8. Draft a message (max 80 words) for the best A or B contact: role, one relevant achievement, already applied.
   No flattery, no referral demand, no repeated follow-up.

Output: job and team clues; Candidates 1-3 (name, current title, grade, evidence, source URL, contact channel);
best contact and why; missing evidence/uncertainty; short outreach message.
If no A or B contact: "No reliable public hiring lead found. Apply through the official page and use the listed
recruiting contact."
